Risk & Compliance - Department Procedures

ACL TEST TYPE: DEPARTMENT ONLY
AUTHORIZED DEPARTMENT: Risk & Compliance

Purpose
This document describes department-level procedures for managing enterprise risk and
compliance activities.

Department Procedures
Risk & Compliance maintains the enterprise risk register, coordinates regulatory
obligations, reviews internal control exceptions, and tracks policy-related remediation.

Test Facts
The department's quarterly risk review uses the marker RISK-COMP-Q4-118.
The quarterly review covers risk ownership, mitigation status, control effectiveness,
and outstanding remediation actions.

This document is intended for department-level ACL testing.
