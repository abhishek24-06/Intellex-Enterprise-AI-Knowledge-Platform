# Procurement Compliance Controls

**ACL TEST TYPE:** TWO DEPARTMENTS ONLY

**Authorized departments:**
1. Risk & Compliance
2. Finance

## Purpose

This document describes controls used jointly by Risk & Compliance and Finance for
high-value procurement reviews.

## Process

Risk & Compliance reviews the control framework, approval evidence, segregation of
duties, and policy exceptions. Finance validates budget availability, approval records,
invoice references, and financial coding.

## Test Facts

The joint review process uses the marker `RC-FIN-PROC-204`.
A procurement exception is considered open until both departmental reviews are
completed and the remediation owner is recorded.

This document is intentionally restricted to two departments for multi-entry ACL testing.
