# Apple Employee Handbook 2026

## Welcome to Apple

Welcome to Apple. Our mission is to create products that enrich people's lives through innovation, design, and privacy.

## Working Hours

- Monday to Friday
- Standard working hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Hybrid work policy available for eligible teams.

## Core Values

- Innovation
- Simplicity
- Privacy
- Accessibility
- Collaboration

## Information Security

Every employee must:

- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Use company-managed Apple devices.
- Never share confidential source code outside Apple.
- Report suspicious emails to the Security Team immediately.

## Leave Policy

Employees receive:

- 20 Annual Leave days
- 12 Sick Leave days
- 10 Company Holidays

## Employee Benefits

- Apple Health Insurance
- Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
- Annual Learning Budget
- Mental Wellness Support
- Apple Product Discounts
