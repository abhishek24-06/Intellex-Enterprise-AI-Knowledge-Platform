# Operations - Supply Chain Team

**Access level:** ORGANIZATIONAL - everyone in the organization

## Team mission
The Supply Chain Team keeps materials, products, and supplier information moving reliably from planning through fulfillment.

## What the team owns
- Procurement coordination and supplier follow-up
- Inbound and outbound logistics coordination
- Inventory planning and replenishment
- Delivery-status visibility and operational escalation
- Cross-functional coordination with Operations, Finance, Sales, and Engineering

## Standard workflow
1. Review demand and current inventory.
2. Identify replenishment or supplier risks.
3. Confirm orders, lead times, and delivery commitments.
4. Monitor inbound and outbound movement.
5. Escalate material risks with a concise status and requested action.
6. Close the loop by updating the operational record.

## Key performance indicators
- On-time delivery
- Inventory accuracy
- Stock-out rate
- Supplier lead-time adherence
- Open supply-risk items

## Visibility rule
This document is organizational and is safe for all employees to access. It contains general operating guidance for the Operations Department and Supply Chain Team.
