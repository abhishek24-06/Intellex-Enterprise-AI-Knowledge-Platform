1 | FaceForensics++: Learning to Detect Manipulated Facial Images (2019) | Andreas Rössler, Davide Cozzolino, Luisa Verdoliva, Christian Riess, Justus Thies, Matthias Nießner | Introduced a large-scale benchmark dataset for detecting manipulated facial images and evaluated various CNN architectures. | Standardized benchmark dataset; enables reproducible comparison across models; supports multiple manipulation types and compression levels. | Focuses mainly on early manipulation methods; detectors trained on it often overfit and fail to generalize to unseen generators.

Delp | Uses CNN to extract spatial frame features from video frames and LSTM to learn temporal inconsistencies. | Clear CNN to LSTM pipeline; strong temporal modeling; easy to implement. | Uses older CNN architectures; comparatively lower accuracy.
8 | Deepfake Video Detection Using Recurrent Neural Networks (IEEE AVSS) | Sabir et al. | CNN-based frame feature extraction followed by LSTM for video-level classification. | Direct CNN + LSTM integration; effective video-level detection. | High computational cost; longer training time.
9 | Combining CNN and LSTM for Video-Based Face Forgery Detection (Elsevier Neurocomputing) | Zhou et al. | Hybrid CNN + LSTM framework for spatial and temporal forgery detection. | Strong spatio-temporal learning; improved accuracy. | More complex pipeline; higher memory usage.
10 | Deepfake Detection Using Spatiotemporal Features (IEEE Access) | Li et al. | Joint CNN and LSTM model to capture spatial and temporal artifacts in videos. | Explicit spatio-temporal modeling; robust detection. | Dataset-dependent performance; generalization issues.

12 | A Survey: Deepfake and Current Technologies for Solutions (2024) | Sayan Banerjee, Sumit Kumar Yadav, Ankit Dhara, Md Ajij | Reviews detection methods including forensics, ML, multimodal approaches, and blockchain authentication. | Broad coverage including technical and societal aspects. | Real-time detection remains unsolved.
